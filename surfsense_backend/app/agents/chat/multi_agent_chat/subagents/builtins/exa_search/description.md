Exa web search specialist: searches the open web with Exa, on this workspace's own Exa key, and returns up to 10 results per search (title, URL, a short matching passage, site and date). Answers in seconds.
Use it for public facts, current events, finding pages or sources on a topic, and checking a claim against the web, and always when the user picked Exa. Results are short passages, not whole pages: to read a page in full, hand its URL to a page-reading specialist when one is listed. Not for Google's own results (rankings, AI overview, People also ask: the Google Search specialist), places (Google Maps) or videos (YouTube).
