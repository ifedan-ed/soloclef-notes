The bell at the bottom of the rail opens **Notifications**. A red number on
it counts what you haven't read. You can also open it from your avatar menu:
**Notifications**. On a phone, it is in the menu sheet. The new
[phone app](/docs/mobile-app) has the same list under its **Inbox** tab, and
can tell you on the lock screen when something finished (see below).

## What you are told about

- **Mentions and replies:** someone mentions you in a comment, or replies to
  yours. Click it to go to the notebook and the comment. Comments aren't
  available in notebook chats for now.
- **Status:** a source has finished indexing or failed, a connector has
  finished syncing, or a file was not read because your credits ran out.
- **Announcements:** news from the site's administrators, such as a new
  feature or planned maintenance.

The tabs filter the list: **All**, **Mentions**, **Announcements** and
**Unread**. **Mark all read** clears the count.

To delete a notification, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Delete**. **Clear
all** deletes everything on the tab you are on (on **Unread**, only the unread
ones, older pages included), once you confirm. Deleting is for you alone: it
goes from your other open tabs and your phone too, and an announcement stays
in everyone else's Notifications. A file or sync still in progress stays until
it has finished.

Notifications stay in the app: none are sent by email. The results of
[automations](/docs/automations) don't come here; with **Email me the
result** on, they come by email instead.

## On your phone

The phone app shows the same list under **Inbox**, with the same tabs,
**Mark all read** and **Clear all**, and a number on the tab for what you
haven't read. Tapping an item opens what it is about: a mention opens the
notebook's chat, an announcement follows its link, a credits notice opens
**Plan and credits**. Swipe an item to the left, or press and hold it, to
delete it. What you delete on the website leaves the app within a minute.

With the app installed and notifications allowed, your phone can also tell you
on the lock screen when something finished while you weren't looking: an
answer that took a while, a step waiting for your approval, a Studio output,
an automation run, a source, a mention or reply, an announcement, an accepted
invite, or credits running out. The line is generic ("Your audio overview is
ready") and never shows the content; tapping it opens the right place in the
app. Each kind has its own switch under **More → Notifications** in the app,
and the app only asks for permission after your first answer or output,
never when it starts.

## Announcements

An announcement has a title and a short message, and sometimes a link.

- **Open** follows the link: a page in the app opens in place, a web address
  opens in a new tab.
- **Mark read** clears it from the count without opening anything.

An announcement can have an end date, after which it disappears on its own.
If the administrators withdraw one, it disappears from everyone's
Notifications straight away.

## Sending announcements (administrators)

Site administrators have an **Announcements** page under **User settings**.

1. Write a **Title** and a **Message**. The message can use a little
   formatting: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `[text](link)`, and lines starting
   with `- ` for bullets. The **Preview** shows it as recipients will see it.
2. Add a **Link** (a web address, or a page in the app such as `/docs`) and an
   **Expires** date if you want them.
3. Under **Send to**, choose **Everyone** (every account) or **One
   workspace** (that workspace's members only).
4. Choose **Send**. It arrives in each person's Notifications at once, unread.

The **Sent** list shows each announcement, who it went to, and how many have
read it (someone who read it and then deleted it still counts). **Withdraw**
takes it out of everyone's Notifications; the record stays in the list.
