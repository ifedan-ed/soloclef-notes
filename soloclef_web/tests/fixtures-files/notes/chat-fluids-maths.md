# Maintenance fluids: the 4-2-1 rule

For a child of weight $w$ kg, the hourly maintenance rate is

$$
R(w) = \begin{cases}
4w & w \le 10 \\
40 + 2(w - 10) & 10 < w \le 20 \\
60 + (w - 20) & w > 20
\end{cases}
$$

so a 24 kg child gets $60 + 4 = 64$ ml/h.[^nice]

| Weight (kg) | ml/h | ml/day |
|--:|--:|--:|
| 8 | 32 | 768 |
| 15 | 50 | 1200 |
| 24 | 64 | 1536 |

Use isotonic fluid with 5% glucose; check sodium daily [citation:2001].

[^nice]: NICE NG29 now recommends calculating by Holliday–Segar and restricting to 80% in some cases.
