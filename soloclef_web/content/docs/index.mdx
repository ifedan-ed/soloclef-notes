<BrandName /> is a notebook for study and research. Add your material to a
notebook, ask questions and get answers that cite the exact passage, then
turn your sources into reports, slide decks, podcasts, videos, flashcards
and quizzes.

![A notebook: sources on the left, the chat in the middle, Studio on the right](/docs/screens/notebook.png)
