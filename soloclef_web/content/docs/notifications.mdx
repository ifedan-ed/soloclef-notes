The bell near the bottom of the left rail opens Notifications, your inbox. A
red number on it counts what you haven't read. On a phone browser it is in
the menu sheet, and the [phone app](/docs/mobile-app) has the same list under
its Inbox tab.

## What you are told about

- A Studio output is ready, or could not be made. Opening it opens the
  notebook's Studio. A failed output has Try again.
- A source could not be added or read. A source that is ready is only
  reported when it was large, 20 MB or more, or took over two minutes.
- An [automation](/docs/automations) run finished, failed, needs your attention
  or timed out. Opening it opens the run. Cancelled runs are not reported.
- A [mail draft](/docs/email#drafts-waiting) is waiting for you to send it.
  Opening it opens Drafts waiting.

Several of one kind close together become one notification, such as "10
imports failed". Each Studio output gets its own.

## The inbox

All and Unread filter the list. Mark all read clears the count. To delete a
notification, open its menu and choose Delete. Clear all deletes everything
on the tab you are on, once you confirm. What you delete goes from your other
tabs and your phone too.

Notifications are not sent by email. An automation can email you its result
separately, with Email me the result.

## Pushes to your phone

With the phone app installed and notifications allowed, each notification
also reaches your phone. A push says only what happened, such as "Slide deck
ready" or "A mail draft is waiting for you", and at most the notebook's name.
It never shows an answer, a file name or anything from your mail. Tapping it
opens the right place in the app.

Nothing is pushed while you are looking at <BrandName />, in a browser tab or in
the app. A burst of notifications pushes once.

Under Settings, Notifications on the website you choose:

- Send a push for: one switch each for Studio outputs, Sources, Automations
  and Mail drafts. All are on until you turn them off.
- Quiet hours: off by default. Switched on, no pushes are sent between From
  and Until, in the time zone you pick. The span may run overnight, such as
  22:00 to 07:00.

The phone app has the same per-kind switches under More, Notifications.
Quiet hours are set on the website. The inbox always keeps everything,
whatever you switch off.

Pushes need the server to have them switched on. If you never get one, ask
the site's administrator.
