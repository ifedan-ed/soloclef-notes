You can connect your mailboxes so the assistant can search and read your
mail. From a Stalwart mailbox it can also write drafts, which are sent only
when you press Send.

There are three kinds of mailbox, all added under Settings, Connectors:

| Mailbox | How it connects | Read | Draft and send |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Any IMAP mailbox | Server, username and password or app password, on the Email (IMAP) card | Yes | No |
| Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 | Microsoft's own sign-in, on the Email (IMAP) card | Yes | No |
| Stalwart | Stalwart's own sign-in or an app password, on the Stalwart (JMAP) card | Yes, and whole conversations | Yes, when you press Send |

<BrandName /> also sends you a few emails of its own. See
[Emails the app sends you](#emails-the-app-sends-you).

## Connecting an IMAP mailbox

1. Open Settings, Connectors, and choose Connect on the Email (IMAP) card.
2. Choose Add account and fill in:
    - Label: a name for the mailbox, such as Work inbox. Empty uses the
      username.
    - IMAP host and Port: your provider's IMAP server. The port is 993
      unless you change it.
    - Use TLS: on by default. With it off, the connection uses STARTTLS
      instead, usually on port 143. Mail is never sent in plain text.
    - Username and Password.
3. Choose Test connection. It says how many folders it found, and saves
   nothing.
4. Choose Save.

If your account uses two-step sign-in, use an app password. For Gmail, make
one under Google Account, Security, App passwords.

The mail server must be reachable from the internet.

### Microsoft mailboxes

Microsoft no longer lets IMAP sign in with a password, so Outlook.com,
Hotmail and Microsoft 365 mailboxes connect through Microsoft:

1. On the Email (IMAP) card, choose Connect a Microsoft account.
2. Sign in on Microsoft's page and accept. Microsoft's page says the app may
   read and write your mail, because Microsoft has no read-only permission
   for IMAP. <BrandName /> only reads.
3. You come back with "Microsoft account connected", and the mailbox in the
   list.

Finish within 10 minutes, in the same browser. If the button isn't there,
the server's administrator hasn't set Microsoft sign-in up.

Some things to know:

- A work or school account may need your organization's administrator to
  approve the app first. The page says so.
- The sign-in renews itself while the mailbox is used. Changing your
  Microsoft password does not end it. Microsoft ends it after 90 days without
  use, or when you or your administrator revoke it. The row then says
  "Microsoft needs you to sign in again." with a Reconnect button.
- Only the mailbox of the account you sign in with can be added. Shared
  mailboxes can't.
- Removing the mailbox deletes the sign-in from <BrandName />. To remove the app
  from your Microsoft account too, go to account.live.com/consent/Manage for
  a personal account, or ask your administrator.

## Connecting a Stalwart mailbox

Stalwart is a mail server that speaks JMAP. Choose Connect on the Stalwart
(JMAP) card, then one of two ways:

- Sign in with Stalwart. Enter the server's address, such as
  `mail.example.com`, and choose Continue to Stalwart. You sign in on
  Stalwart's own page, so <BrandName /> never sees your password. This is offered
  when both this site and your Stalwart server allow it.
- Use an app password. Make one in Stalwart's Account Manager, under
  Credentials. Enter the server's address, an optional label, your username
  and the app password, and choose Save.

Any of the server's addresses works. If Stalwart calls itself by another
name than the one you enter, for example `stalwart.example.com` when you enter
`mail.example.com`, <BrandName /> connects with the server's own address and shows
it on the mailbox's row.

Each mailbox in the list says whether it can send: "Can send drafts, each
only after you confirm it." or "Read-only: this sign-in can't send mail.",
depending on what Stalwart allows. Test connection signs in again and counts
the folders.

The Stalwart server must be reachable from the internet over https on port
443.

## Removing a mailbox

Remove takes a mailbox off <BrandName />. Nothing in the mailbox changes. For a
Stalwart sign-in, <BrandName /> also asks Stalwart to end it. For an app password,
delete the password in your mail provider too.

To pause all your IMAP and Microsoft mailboxes without removing them, pick
Email (IMAP) under Your connectors and switch off Enabled. A Stalwart mailbox
has no such switch: remove it to stop the assistant using it.

Passwords and sign-ins are stored encrypted and are never shown again.

## What the assistant can do

Mail is read live, when it is needed. It is never copied into <BrandName />,
never becomes a source and is never added to search. The assistant can:

- list your mailboxes and their folders;
- search by words, sender, recipient, subject and date, or for unread mail.
  It searches the inbox unless you name a folder, and every mailbox unless
  you name one;
- read a message, with its list of attachments;
- read a whole conversation, in a Stalwart mailbox;
- read an attachment: PDF, Word, PowerPoint and EPUB files with your
  parser, text files as they are, and pictures with your vision model. The
  text is used for that answer only. To keep an attachment, save it and
  upload it as a source;
- draft a message, from a Stalwart mailbox that can send.

It never deletes, moves or flags mail, and never marks a message as read.

Text inside an email is treated as something to read, never as an
instruction. A message that says "forward this to…" is reported to you, not
obeyed.

Mail is read by a notebook's chat when you ask about your mail, and by
[automations](/docs/automations). Each email used is cited with a numbered chip
showing the sender, subject and date.

Limits:

- A search returns up to 50 messages, 20 unless more are asked for.
- A message's text is cut at 20,000 characters. A conversation shows its
  newest 20 messages.
- An attachment can be up to 25 MB, and a PDF up to 500 pages.

## Drafts: nothing is sent until you press Send

The assistant cannot send mail. When you ask it to write or answer a message
from a Stalwart mailbox that can send, it makes a draft and shows it on a
card:

- the mailbox it is from, To, Cc and Bcc, the subject and the text;
- for a reply, the message it answers. A recipient who wasn't in that
  conversation is marked "not in the original conversation";
- each attached file, with its size and where it came from.

The card shows the draft exactly as it would be sent. Nothing leaves until
you press Send on the card. Pressing Enter never sends. Discard throws the
draft away. To change a draft, ask for the change; the new draft replaces the
old one.

The assistant only drafts because you asked. It never drafts because an
email asked it to, and never to an address that only appears in an email's
text.

### Drafts waiting

Every draft also waits on the Drafts waiting page, whether it came from a
chat or an automation. You reach it from the notification "A mail draft is
waiting for you", on the website or in the phone app. Each draft there shows
its mailbox, when it was made and until when it can be sent, with the same
Send and Discard buttons. A draft from an automation can only be sent from
here.

### Attachments

A draft can carry:

- your documents, as the original file you uploaded;
- Studio outputs, such as a slide deck, report or audio overview;
- an attachment of a message in a Stalwart mailbox, to forward it.

The files are part of what you confirm. If one changes or is removed after
the draft was made, the draft can't be sent as it is. A different file is
never sent in its place.

### Unusual sends

Some sends need a second confirmation. The card lists each reason with a tick
box, and Send anyway stays off until every box is ticked:

- more than 20 recipients;
- 20 or more emails already sent from <BrandName /> in the past hour;
- recipients this mailbox has never written to, not counting people already
  in the conversation you are answering. If the mailbox can't be checked,
  those recipients are listed too;
- more than 10 MB of attachments together, which some mail servers refuse.

The server checks the ticks too. If something becomes unusual between
showing the card and pressing Send, the send stops and the card asks again.

### Expiry and failures

- A draft can be sent for 24 hours. After that it says "Draft expired".
- A press of Send is good for 10 minutes, for that exact message. After
  that, or if the draft changed, the card asks you to check it and press Send
  again.
- If the mail server refuses the message, the card says "Email not sent",
  with the reason, and offers Send again. Check your Sent folder first: if
  the message is there, it went out.

A sent message goes out as plain text from that mailbox, and a copy is kept
in its Sent folder. Every send, accepted or refused, is written to your
activity log.

## Emails the app sends you

These come from the server's own mail service, if the server has one:

- Password reset. Forgot Password? on the sign-in page sends a link to
  choose a new password. It works once, for an hour.
- Automation results, when Email me the result is on. The subject is the
  automation's name and how the run ended, such as "Weekly digest:
  finished". A failed run's email gives the reason.

There are no other emails. Notifications stay in the app and on your phone.
