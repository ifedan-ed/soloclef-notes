<BrandName /> has an app for iPhone and Android. It signs in to the same server and
account as the website, so your notebooks are the same everywhere.

## Getting it

- iPhone: [<BrandName /> on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6821393434).
- Android: there is no Play Store listing yet. Ask the site's administrator
  for the APK file.

On a phone browser, the website works too, with Sources, Chat and Studio
tabs.

## Signing in

The sign-in screen shows which server it will use, with Change server below
it. To use your own server, choose Change server and enter its address, such
as `notes.example.org`. The app checks that it is a <BrandName /> server and that
it is reachable over https. Changing servers later signs you out of the old
one and removes everything the app kept from it.

If the server has single sign-on, its button comes first. The email and
password form is under Other ways to sign in. Forgot password, Create an
account and Request access open the website, when the server offers them.

The app stays signed in while you use it, and signs out after 180 days
without use. See [Account and security](/docs/account-and-security#staying-signed-in).

## What it does

The app has five tabs: Notebooks, Notes, Automations, Inbox and More.

### Notebooks

Browse, search, create, rename, pin, copy and delete notebooks. The search
also looks inside sources and notes.

In a notebook:

- Sources: tick sources on and off, read source guides, and add sources from
  files, photos, the camera, a folder, your library, Google Drive, Nextcloud,
  a link, or a web search with Fast or Deep research. Uploads offer the same
  Vision switch and parsers as the website. Each source's … menu has Remove
  from notebook.
- Chat: answers with citations you can tap, suggested and follow-up
  questions, @ for sources and / for prompts, the model picker, Tools, and
  the approval cards for tools marked Ask first. Under an answer: Copy,
  Download as Markdown, Save to note and Regenerate.
- Studio: every output kind, with the same options as the website. Audio
  plays in the background with lock-screen controls, at 0.75× to 2× speed,
  with a mini player that keeps playing when you leave the page. Video plays
  full screen or picture in picture. Reports, decks, documents and tables
  open in the app. Share publicly makes a public link, and Keep offline
  keeps an output on the phone.

### Notes

Your [notes](/docs/notes), with search, pinned notes first, and an editor with
a Markdown toolbar and a preview. Notes save as you type.

### Automations

Your [automations](/docs/automations), their runs, and a form to make or change
one.

### Inbox

Your [notifications](/docs/notifications), with an unread count on the tab. Swipe
an item left to delete it. Tapping a mail draft's notification opens Drafts
waiting, where you can check the draft and press Send or Discard.

### More

- Settings: Connectors, Automations, Models, System instructions, Prompts,
  Memory, Activity log and Public links.
- You: Profile, Appearance, Notifications, API access, Connect your agent,
  Signed-in devices, Offline and storage, and App lock.
- Server: the server's address, to change it.
- Help: Help and docs, Report an issue, and the server's privacy policy.
- Sign out.

Connectors in the app covers Google Drive, Nextcloud, Email (IMAP) with
Microsoft sign-in, and MCP servers. Stalwart mailboxes and Exa and Tavily
keys are added on the website.

App lock asks for Face ID, Touch ID or the fingerprint sensor before the app
shows anything, and can hide the app's contents in the app switcher.

Report an issue opens your mail app with a report addressed to the server's
contact address.

## Working offline

Outputs you keep offline open without a connection. Changes you make offline,
such as note edits, source switches and quiz progress, wait on the phone and
are sent when you are back online. Chat questions are never queued. If the
server refuses a change, it is listed under More, Not saved, with the reason.

## Notifications on the phone

The app asks to show notifications after your first answer or output, not
when it starts. Under More, Notifications you choose which kinds are pushed.
Quiet hours are set on the website. A push says only what happened, never
what is in it.
