A workspace holds notebooks, documents and settings. You can have several,
switch between them from the rail, and invite other people into one.

## Members

Open your avatar menu → **Workspace** → **Manage members**. The **Members**
table shows each person's name, when they last signed in, and their role. The
Owner can change a member's role (**Make Editor**, **Make Viewer**) or
**Remove** them.

### Inviting people

Owners and Editors can **Invite** someone, in one of two ways:

- **By email:** enter their address. **Send invitation** emails them a link
  (see [Email](/docs/email#emails-the-app-sends-you)). If the email can't be
  sent, copy the link and send it to them yourself. Only the account with that
  address can accept it.
- **Invite link:** a link that anyone with an account here can use to join.
  **Maximum uses** limits how many people can use it; it is unlimited by
  default.

Either way, choose the role they get, **Editor** or **Viewer**, and when the
invitation expires: **In 7 days** (the default), **In 30 days** or **Never**.
A **Note** is optional.

The people you invite need an account here already, because sign-ups are
closed. The invitation opens a page where they choose **Sign in to accept**,
then **Accept invitation** or **Not now**.

**Pending invitations** are listed under the members, with **Resend** (at most
once a minute) and **Revoke**. Expired ones are listed separately.

## Roles

Every workspace has three roles, listed under **Workspace settings → Team
Roles**. Custom roles are not available.

- **Owner:** everything, including members, settings and public links. The
  person who made the workspace is its Owner; ownership is never given
  through an invitation or a role change.
- **Editor:** adds sources, chats, makes Studio outputs and notes, adds
  connectors, sets up automations, changes the workspace's models, and invites
  Editors or Viewers. An Editor can't delete notebooks, documents, Studio
  outputs, automations or chat history, change roles, edit General settings or
  System Instructions, switch a connection off, or create public links.
- **Viewer:** reads the workspace's notebooks, sources, notes, Studio outputs
  and automations. A Viewer can't chat, add sources or make outputs.

Members share connections too. In a workspace, the assistant can use the
Google Drive, Nextcloud and Email connections of every member, except the
ones their owner switched off there (see
[Notebooks and sources](/docs/notebooks-and-sources#connectors)).

## Public links

Owners can share something read-only with anyone who has the link, without
signing in:

- **Share chat publicly…** in the notebook menu shares a copy of the chat as
  it is now.
- **Share publicly**, in a finished Studio output's viewer, shares a copy of
  that output. It opens as you see it: a mind map to explore, flashcards and
  a quiz to study (a visitor's answers stay in their browser), a slide deck's
  slides, a data table's sheet, a report with its contents. Your prompt and
  sources, **Explain** and a report's recommended outputs are not shown.
  Sharing it again while it is unchanged gives the same link, with its
  current title and the expiry you pick.
- **Share notebook…** in the notebook menu makes a live link to the notebook.
  Visitors see your newest chat in it, its finished Studio outputs and its
  source files, as they are each time the link is opened. Notes are not
  shown. The notebook then shows **Shared**. **New address** replaces the
  link, and **Stop sharing** turns it off.

A link expires **After 7 days** (the default), **After 30 days** or
**Never**. An expired link says "This link has expired".

**Workspace settings → Public links** lists every link (Chat, Output or Live
notebook), with **Copy link**, **Open link** and **Revoke**. Revoking stops a
link at once.

## Workspace settings

Open your avatar menu → **Workspace** → **Workspace settings**. Everyone in the
workspace can open every page; what they can change depends on their role.

- **General:** the workspace's **Name** and **Description**, and **API key
  access** for agents (see [Connect your agent](/docs/connect-your-agent));
  only the Owner can change these. **Export knowledge base** downloads every
  document in the workspace as a ZIP of Markdown files.
- **Connectors:** Google Drive, Nextcloud, Email and MCP servers. See
  [Notebooks and sources](/docs/notebooks-and-sources#connectors) and
  [Email](/docs/email).
- **Automations:** see [Automations](/docs/automations).
- **Models:** the model each job uses by default. See
  [Chat and citations](/docs/chat-and-citations#choosing-the-model).
- **Team Roles:** the three roles above.
- **System Instructions:** see below.
- **Public links:** see above.

### System Instructions

System Instructions shape every answer in the workspace, in chat and in
Studio: tone, audience, format and length. Answers still come only from your
sources, keep their citations and follow the safety rules. A line that tries
to change those is ignored, and saving warns "Some instructions won't be
followed". Instructions can be up to 4,000 characters, and only the Owner can
change them. While they are set, the chat shows **Instructions applied**.

## Leaving or deleting a workspace

In your avatar menu → **Workspace**, the Owner can **Delete workspace** and
everyone else can **Leave workspace**. Deleting a workspace deletes all its
documents and chats; your Google Drive, Nextcloud and Email connections stay
with your account. You can't delete or leave your only workspace: create or
join another one first.

Each person can own up to 400 workspaces.
